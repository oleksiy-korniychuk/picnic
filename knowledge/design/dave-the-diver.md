# What the inspiration is

The game loop and systems in Dave the Diver are a great inspiration for Picnic:[^board-2026-07-03]

* Unlike other extraction games, DtD has multiple locations you can go outside of the extraction area (The Boat, The Sushi Bar, the Fish Farm).
* The pacing and interweaving of story beats into these locations adds to the continued sense of progress and breaks up the routine of the core game cycle (go in, extract, upgrade, repeat).
* These beats are also used as natural checkpoints to evolve the locations, the mechanics, and to switch up the gameplay cycle itself.
* This keeps the challenge up but different, as older mechanics are optimized out with QoL to be replaced by newer ones.

# How this maps to Picnic

Interpretation - how the DtD lessons apply to the current design:

* The [base hub](/design/progression.md) (stash management + contracts) is today's single "home" location; the hideout upgrade and [apprentice system](/design/apprentices.md) already add home-side depth in the DtD spirit.
* Future direction: multiple out-of-Zone locations (for example a hideout and a black market) instead of one hub, with story beats woven in as pacing and as natural checkpoints that evolve locations and mechanics over the course of a campaign.
* Mechanic evolution: as systems mature, QoL can optimize away older mechanics (for example manual loadout juggling) to make room for newer ones, keeping the challenge fresh rather than merely harder.

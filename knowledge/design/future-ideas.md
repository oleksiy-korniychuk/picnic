# Unassessed ideas

## Meta AI director

A future feature twist, once the game is stable and fun: a meta AI that collects data about how players are playing the game, reasons about it, and then releases tweaks and updates to the game's rules to keep gameplay fresh.[^board-2025-11-14]

## Grid-distortion tile VFX

If an anomaly is present, do not show the anomaly; instead, randomly change the color or character of the surrounding tiles every few turns - for example, a patch of grass near a "Witches Jelly" anomaly might flicker to a slightly different green or turn into a `?` for one frame, forcing the player to watch the screen like a hawk.[^board-2025-11-30]

Not yet assessed against the [radius-based hunch direction](/design/hunch.md); both address hidden-anomaly perception and may complement or conflict.
